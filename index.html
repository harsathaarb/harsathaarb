About

Both sides of the table.

Penetration testing taught me how applications and APIs fail: SQL injection, XSS, insecure uploads and broken object-level authorization. Forensic work taught me what an attacker leaves behind in memory, on disk and in logs.

In security operations and product engineering, I use both. I triage alerts in Splunk, Wazuh and CrowdStrike Falcon, map activity to MITRE ATT&CK, audit enterprise configurations across endpoint, identity and cloud platforms, and write findings that someone else can act on.

I also run DEFCON Coimbatore events, because the community that shares what it learns gets better at defending.
